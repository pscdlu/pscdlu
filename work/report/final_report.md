# Final report: Unit I Teaching Aptitude study guide

Files: `outputs/UGC_NET_P1_Unit1_Teaching_Aptitude_Study_Guide.docx` and `.pdf` (made from the DOCX), `outputs/working_files.zip`. Supporting files: `coverage_map.md`, `verification_log.md`, `booklet_conflicts_raw.md`.

## Size
365 A4 pages; about 138,000 words in the PDF (about 123,000 in the six chapters). 6 chapters, 45 sections, 320 "Check yourself" questions.

## Coverage
- All 1,000 booklet questions are mapped to a section. A script confirms that each appears in a writer's coverage note, which names the passage that teaches its answer. The 25 Teaching Aptitude questions of Booklet 0 are also covered.
- Two independent blind checks answered all 320 "Check yourself" questions from the section text before looking at the keys. All 320 matched. Every worked calculation was recomputed and found right.
- Every row of the coverage map has a section; full table in `coverage_map.md`. Rows I could not cover fully:
  - **6.5 Reading test scores:** real-paper evidence is "rare". It is taught fully because 11 booklet questions need it.
  - **The Hindi syllabus:** not printed, because the official Hindi text could not be read.
  - **Exam footprint lines:** based only on search summaries of candidates' recollections and coaching analyses, because no NTA paper could be opened. The format percentages from the survey were left out as unsupported.

## Verification
- Every writer logged each fact with its sources. A verification pass on 20 October 2026 then rechecked every fact first written from memory. The full log is in `verification_log.md`.
- **Limit:** this environment blocked direct access to ugc.gov.in, nta.ac.in, ugcnet.nta.ac.in, swayam.gov.in, pib.gov.in, education.gov.in and egazette.gov.in. Official facts were therefore confirmed from search-result summaries that quote those pages, not from the documents themselves.
- **Unresolved dated fact:** official sources disagree on the number of SWAYAM PRABHA channels. PIB notes of March and June 2026 say 48; the SWAYAM PRABHA website and a PIB factsheet of August 2026 say 40. The guide gives both and tells students to recheck.

## Booklet items found wrong, doubtful or out of date
No answer key had to be overruled. Items to consider revising:

1. **S12-Q15:** treats an "autonomous development level" as Bigge's, which could not be confirmed. S1-Q32 treats the same label as not a level. Reword S12-Q15.
2. **S8-Q16:** the explanation says "the first statement" where it means option (C).
3. **S9-Q6:** "seventeen mental processes" in RCEM is found only in slides and uploads.
4. **S1-Q36, S8-Q44:** Jackson (1968) named only the pre-active and interactive phases; "post-active" was added later (Clark and Peterson, 1986). The S1-Q36 explanation says "Jackson divided teaching into three phases"; it should say that Jackson named two and later writers added the post-active phase. The keys stand.
5. **S13-Q46, S16-Q45:** SWAYAM PRABHA channels (48 or 40) and the tenth SWAYAM National Coordinator (INI). These are dated figures; recheck before the next printing.
6. **S14-Q29:** that ONOS "replaced" e-ShodhSindhu is not confirmed; sources say it builds on it.
7. **S16-Q49, S12-Q33:** the explanations say failed courses count in the SGPA "per the UGC illustration". UGC's printed illustration has no F row; the rule comes from the formula's wording ("all the courses undergone"). **S20-Q23:** whether a cleared F replaces the original SGPA varies by university.
8. **S20-Q26:** the explanation puts the master's degree only at NHEQF level 6.5. Level 7 also holds M.E./M.Tech. after a four-year bachelor's. The key (doctorate at level 8) is unaffected.
9. **S1-Q5:** the year micro-teaching began varies by source (1963 usually; 1959 and 1961 also given). The key (Stanford) is unaffected.
10. Explanations that overstate their sources:
    - **S4-Q15:** Goleman's five labels come from later work, not the 1995 book.
    - **S17-Q20:** the ranking of Bandura's four sources comes from later writers.
    - **S4-Q18, S6-Q18:** RPwD section 16 says governments "shall endeavour", not "require".
    - **S10-Q35:** Koffka's phrase is usually "other than", not "more than".
    - **S6-Q41:** the reliability figure of "about 0.80" is unconfirmed.
    - **S9-Q30:** Glaser's 1963 article was on instructional technology and measurement, not "programmed instruction".
    - **S1-Q39:** the chalkboard is "one of the oldest" aids, not "the oldest".
    - **S4-Q20:** SWAYAM's licence as an open educational resource is not confirmed.
    - **S10-Q13:** the 15–20 minute attention span is conventional advice with a contested research basis.
11. **S19-Q50, S20-Q19:** these are Unit II topics (authorship ethics; random assignment). They are taught briefly in 1.10 and 1.2 with a pointer to Unit II.

## Choices you should know about
- **Review stop:** after your "continue and finish", the planned review stop was skipped.
- **Section numbers:** fixed as in the outline. The Keller Plan / PSI is taught only in 4.4. Analytic and synthetic methods are in 1.6.
- **Booklet index:** each set is a 50-cell grid (question · section), four sets to a page, followed by the same index arranged by section.
- **Contents:** static, with page numbers taken from the PDF. In Word, a heading may fall one page differently, because Word uses Nirmala UI for Hindi where the PDF uses Noto Sans Devanagari.
- **Fonts:** Calibri 11 for English. Hindi is set in Nirmala UI (standard on Windows) in the DOCX and in Noto Sans Devanagari in the PDF; both render conjuncts correctly.
- **Title-page Hindi:** "शिक्षण अभिवृत्ति" is kept as the conventional heading. Section 2.8 tells students that, strictly, aptitude is अभिक्षमता.
- **Facts left out:** some details could not be confirmed and were dropped rather than guessed, for example several first names and years, NAAC's current grading scale, and who coined "Hawthorne effect".
- **Exam cycles:** the guide names no examination cycle or shift. The one historical date kept is that the NTA has conducted the NET since December 2018.
- **Confusable-pairs table:** it collects every "Often confused" box (325 rows, about 15 pages). It is long, but it is the revision tool the study route calls for.
