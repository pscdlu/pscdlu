Unit-I Teaching Aptitude
- Teaching: Concept, Objectives, Levels of teaching (Memory, Understanding and Reflective), Characteristics and basic requirements.
- Learner's characteristics: Characteristics of adolescent and adult learners (Academic, Social, Emotional and Cognitive), Individual differences.
- Factors affecting teaching related to: Teacher, Learner, Support material, Instructional facilities, Learning environment and Institution.
- Methods of teaching in Institutions of higher learning: Teacher centred vs. Learner centred methods; Off-line vs. On-line methods (Swayam, Swayamprabha, MOOCs etc.).
- Teaching Support System: Traditional, Modern and ICT based.
- Evaluation Systems: Elements and Types of evaluation, Evaluation in Choice Based Credit System in Higher education, Computer based testing, Innovations in evaluation systems.

## 2. Exam structure (latest cycle: June 2026)

Official bulletin not downloadable here (nta.ac.in / ugcnet.nta.nic.in unresolvable). From reports of the June 2026 Information Bulletin:
- Notification/bulletin released 29 April 2026 on https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in ; applications 29 Apr–20 May 2026 (reported extension to 23 May). Exam 22–30 June 2026, 85 subjects, two shifts (9:00–12:00, 15:00–18:00). Sources: https://www.pw.live/ugc-net/exams/ugc-net-2026-notification , https://competition.careers360.com/articles/ugc-net-2026
- Paper 1: 50 MCQs × 2 marks = 100 marks. Paper 2: 100 MCQs × 2 = 200. **No negative marking.**
- Duration: 3 hours (180 min) for Papers 1 and 2 together, in a single sitting with no break (no separate Paper 1 time limit; "1 hour" figures in some coaching sites are not NTA's).
- Mode: Computer Based Test (CBT) only. Medium of Paper 1: English and Hindi (bilingual).
- First NTA cycle: December 2018 — held 18–22 Dec 2018, two shifts/day, 235 cities, 598 centres, 85 subjects, 9,56,837 registered; results 5 Jan 2019. Source: MHRD/PIB press release https://www.education.gov.in/sites/upload_files/mhrd/files/PR_UGC_NET_RESULTS.pdf
- First CBT cycle: also December 2018 (NTA's first; earlier CBSE-run cycles up to July 2018 were pen-and-paper). Source: Amar Ujala https://results.amarujala.com/exam-alerts/nta-fulfilling-the-vision-of-government-to-conduct-fair-transparent-exams-javadekar-praises (and PIB release above).

### Flags against WRITER_BRIEF "Shared Hindi terms"
1. **reflective level**: brief has चिंतन स्तर; NTA-derived Hindi questions use **चिंतनशील** (चिंतनशील स्तर). Recommend switching.
2. **Teaching Aptitude = शिक्षण अभिवृत्ति**: this is what Hindi NET material conventionally uses, but technically अभिवृत्ति = attitude and CSTT-style aptitude = अभिक्षमता. Keep the brief's form only if the NTA Hindi PDF confirms it, and add a note in the book that अभिवृत्ति here means aptitude.
3. All other brief terms could not be checked against NTA; none was contradicted by evidence found.
