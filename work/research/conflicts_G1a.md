# Conflicts, doubts and omissions: G1a (sections 1.1 to 1.5)

## General limitation of this session
WebFetch could not resolve any host, and the shared WebSearch budget (200 calls for the whole run) ran out after about 20 of my searches. Many facts in 1.1 to 1.4 therefore rest on standard references not re-checked on the web this session; each is marked NOT WEB-CHECKED in verify_G1a.md. The editor should recheck those rows, in particular the four definitions of teaching (wording and years), Jackson's phases, the RCEM details, the affective sub-levels, and the upper levels of Simpson and Harrow.

## Facts left out because they could not be confirmed
- The number of RCEM mental processes (16 or 17 in different sources).
- The names of Morrison's five types of unit (science, appreciation, practical-arts, language-arts, pure-practice): a search snippet confirmed "units of five types" but not the names; removed from the text.
- Ziller's division of Herbart's "clearness" into analysis and synthesis: removed (not needed by any booklet item).
- Any description of the "autonomous development level" as Bigge's own (see S12-Q15).
