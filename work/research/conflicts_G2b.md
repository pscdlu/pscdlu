## B. Booklet items: wording or keys to note
- S4-Q15 (EI components). The booklet and guide list Goleman's components as self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy, social skills. These labels match common secondary sources (businessballs, anantamias). My recollection, not web-checked, is that the 1995 book names the five domains differently (knowing one's emotions, managing emotions, motivating oneself, recognising emotions in others, handling relationships) and the "self-regulation/social skills" labels come from Goleman's later work (1998). Guide says "usually listed as five". Key (D) Goleman unaffected.
- S4-Q15 / guide: one search source dated the EI term to 1989; the standard citation is Salovey and Mayer (1990). Guide prints 1990.
- S17-Q20 (strongest source of self-efficacy = mastery). Bandura's 1977 abstract lists the four sources without ranking; the ranking comes from his later work and secondary sources. Key (B) stands.
- S4-Q18 explanation says the Act "requires educational institutions to provide inclusive education". Section 16 actually says the appropriate Government and local authorities "shall endeavour" that institutions they fund or recognise provide inclusive education. Guide words it this way. Key (B) 2016 unaffected.
- S6-Q18 explanation: "required by the RPwD Act" - same nuance as above; key unaffected.
- RPwD assent date: most sources 27 Dec 2016, some government pages 28 Dec 2016. Guide prints only "December 2016". No booklet item depends on the day.
- S15-Q33 (Yerkes-Dodson): "optimum lower for difficult tasks" is the standard textbook statement; the 1908 study was on mice and later replication is mixed. Key (C) stands.
- No booklet key in my 92 ids was found to conflict with a verified fact.

## C. Hindi terminology
- The unit title Teaching Aptitude is शिक्षण अभिवृत्ति (shared term list, NTA usage). In psychology books अभिवृत्ति normally means attitude and aptitude is अभिक्षमता. Section 2.8 uses अभिक्षमता for aptitude and अभिवृत्ति for attitude and explains the clash to the student. Editor may wish to harmonise.
