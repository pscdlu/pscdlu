- Location of Hawthorne Works (Cicero, Illinois) not checked; guide says only "in the United States".
- First names withheld where not confirmed this session: Jacobson, Babad, Inbar, Bandura, Tschannen-Moran, Adams, Biddle, Bruner, Puentedura, Mayer, Crowder, Skinner (surnames only printed).
- Lists of Indian national digital schemes (virtual labs, digital library) removed from 3.5 because unverified; cross-reference to 4.6 kept.
