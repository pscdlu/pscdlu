## 1. Booklet items: conflicts, outdated or ambiguous
- **S17-Q2 (minor wording).** The booklet explanation says "rotation or flex" models are "not flipped". In the Christensen Institute taxonomy the flipped classroom is itself a sub-type of the rotation model, so "rotation is not flipped" is loose. The key (C) is correct. The guide says "station rotation or the flex model" are not flipped and, in Going Further, lists the flipped classroom as a rotation sub-type. (Christensen taxonomy not web-checked this session.)
