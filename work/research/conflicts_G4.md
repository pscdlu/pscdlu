## 1. Booklet items: conflicts, outdated or ambiguous
- **S17-Q2 (minor wording).** The booklet explanation says "rotation or flex" models are "not flipped". In the Christensen Institute taxonomy the flipped classroom is itself a sub-type of the rotation model, so "rotation is not flipped" is loose. The key (C) is correct. The guide says "station rotation or the flex model" are not flipped and, in Going Further, lists the flipped classroom as a rotation sub-type. (Christensen taxonomy not web-checked this session.)
- **S13-Q46 (SWAYAM PRABHA channel count; ambiguous, not wrong).** Booklet: 48 channels per MoE/PIB note on PM e-Vidya, March 2026 (key D). Verified: the PIB backgrounder of 10 March 2026 and a PIB Digital India document of 27 June 2026 do say 48; but the SWAYAM PRABHA website (About page) and a later PIB factsheet (data as of 10 August 2026, https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?id=150840) say 40. Older: 32 at launch (PIB, 9 July 2017), later 34 (secondary sources only). Because the question names the March 2026 note, its key stands. The guide gives both 2026 figures in the Fact watch box and tells the student to recheck. The editor should recheck swayamprabha.gov.in before printing; a standalone question "how many channels" would be unsafe.
- **S16-Q45 (ten National Coordinators).** Confirmed by the same PIB March 2026 backgrounder (ten, incl. INI). The BHU-as-INI-coordinator detail rests on a Careers360 news report (secondary); primary MoE notice not found. Older: nine.
- **S14-Q29 (ONOS "succeeding" e-ShodhSindhu).** Booklet explanation: ONOS takes "over the role of earlier consortia such as e-ShodhSindhu". Verified: ONOS approved 25 Nov 2024, operational 1 Jan 2025 (PIB PRID 2077098; onos.gov.in). No official notice was found that e-ShodhSindhu was discontinued; INFLIBNET pages still describe e-ShodhSindhu and describe ONOS as building on it. The guide says ONOS "builds on" e-ShodhSindhu. Key (A) unaffected.
- **S1-Q41 (ShodhShuddhi flagged DATED).** Verified current: ShodhShuddhi continues, now with DrillBit (since 1 Nov 2023; Urkund/Ouriginal 2019-2023), restricted to theses, synopses and research articles via universities. Key unaffected.
- **S15-Q24 / S18-Q2 (credit ceiling).** Verified 40% under the 2021 Regulations (notified 25 March 2021); 20% under the 2016 Regulations (19 July 2016). No later amendment found; a July 2026 Lok Sabha reply (reported by Careers360) still cites 40%. Note: some sources say "40% of credits"; the regulation text says "total courses". Guide uses "total courses".
- **S11-Q34 (parent/host institution).** Terms not checked against the 2021 regulation text (search budget exhausted). Taught from knowledge; recheck.
- **S6-Q11, S9-Q16, Booklet0 P5-Q14 (four quadrants).** Verified as the description in PIB 2026 material. One search digest quoted the UGC-hosted SWAYAM Guidelines 2024 as saying the framework "moved from a 4-quadrant approach to a multi-dimensional ... approach"; a second search could not confirm. Guide mentions the 2024 Guidelines cautiously in Fact watch. Numbering of quadrants III and IV varies between sources (some older documents list "web resources" as a quadrant); the guide teaches the four parts without fixed numbers.
- **S6-Q11 / S13-Q2 (access, equity, quality).** The three principles are standard and on swayam.gov.in/about per my knowledge, but no search digest quoted them this session. Recheck.
- **S10-Q13 (15-20 minute attention span).** The brief flags the research basis as contested (Wilson and Korn 2007; Bradbury 2016). The guide teaches 15-20 minutes as the conventional answer and adds a caution naming Wilson and Korn (2007), unverified this session.
- **S2-Q19 (SWAYAM NCs listed in explanation as "also AICTE, IIMB, NITTTR").** Consistent with verified list; INI omitted in the booklet's list. No conflict with key.
