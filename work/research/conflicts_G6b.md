## Booklet items: checked against verified facts
- S16-Q49, S12-Q33 (FLAG: DOUBT, F/Ab credits in SGPA): verified that the UGC formula divides by "the sum of the number of credits of all the courses undergone by a student" (UGC CBCS guidelines via search). The UGC's printed illustration (139/20 = 6.95) contains no F row, so "per the UGC illustration" is not literally right; the basis is the formula wording. Guide teaches: keep F/Ab credits in the denominator when applying the UGC formula; says universities differ. Booklet answers stand.
- S20-Q23 (FLAG: DOUBT, cleared grade replaces F in same SGPA): not addressed by UGC guidelines; university practice varies. Guide teaches the arithmetic "when the new grade replaces F" with a caution. Booklet answer stands as arithmetic.
- S4-Q23, S14-Q2 (FLAG: DATED, credit per week): verified 2015 definition (one hour lecture/tutorial or two hours practical per week). CCFUP December 2022 per-semester wording (15/30 hours) and NCrF (30 notional hours per credit) written from knowledge, RECHECK. Both give the same booklet answers.
- S7-Q45, S17-Q40, S12-Q43, S6-Q26 (FLAG: DATED, course categories): 2015 categories verified (AECC = Environmental Science and English/MIL Communication; GE from unrelated discipline; dissertation/project as elective with advisory support). Guide presents 2015 scheme as the CBCS answer and adds a CCFUP 2022 table (RECHECK). Booklet answers stand.
- S8-Q17, S9-Q34, S11-Q8, S13-Q39 (grade scale): verified O/A+/A/B+/B/C/P/F/Ab with 10/9/8/7/6/5/4/0/0. Note: one university-hosted copy (IARE) prints "S (Superior)" for the top grade; that is an institutional adaptation, not the UGC text (UGC-hosted and other copies show O). Guide also states there is no D grade.
- S20-Q26 (FLAG: DOUBT, NHEQF master's levels): booklet explanation lists only 6.5 for master's. Guide teaches 6.5 for the general master's (2 years after 3-year bachelor's or 1 year after 4-year bachelor's) and 7 for a 2-year master's after a 4-year bachelor's (e.g. M.E./M.Tech.), and PG diploma at 6. This is from knowledge of the NHEQF table and could NOT be web-checked: RECHECK exact level 6/6.5/7 entries. The booklet's keyed answer (doctorate = level 8) is unaffected.
- S19-Q1 (exit awards) and S19-Q10 (ABC vs NAD): consistent with NEP 2020/CCFUP; RECHECK (not web-checked).
- S1-Q38 (FLAG: DATED): guide names the 2015 guidelines and the later CCFUP 2022 framework.
