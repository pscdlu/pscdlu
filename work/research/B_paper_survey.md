# B. Survey of UGC NET Paper 1, Unit I (Teaching Aptitude): what is asked and in what format

Scope: NTA-conducted cycles from December 2018 to June 2026 (and its re-examination, if any).
Compiled 19 October 2026. Work in progress; sections are filled progressively.

## Method and limits

- In this session, WebFetch failed with DNS errors on every site tried (testbook.com, jrfadda.com, careers360.com, pw.live, adda247.com), and direct curl downloads were refused by the proxy (403). Nothing could be downloaded to the scratchpad `dl/` folders, so no NTA PDF was opened.
- The evidence therefore comes from WebSearch result digests: search-engine summaries of memory-based analyses, coaching blogs, and book listings. Each claim below names the URL the digest drew on. These sources report what candidates recalled after the exam. They are not authorities on facts and they sometimes recycle older text.
- Question text is not reproduced; item types are described in my own words.

### Multi-year claims (coaching analyses)
- **Topics claimed to recur across 2019-2025:** levels of teaching (memory, understanding, reflective); teacher-centred vs learner-centred methods; formative vs summative evaluation; learner characteristics; Bloom's taxonomy; effective teaching; motivation; teaching vs indoctrination vs conditioning; CBCS; NRT vs CRT; SWAYAM/MOOCs. Sources: https://blog.ipleaders.in/ugc-net-paper-1-previous-year-question/ ; https://www.oliveboard.in/blog/teaching-aptitude-for-ugc-net/ ; https://lawsikho.com/blog/ugc-net-paper-1-important-topics/ ; https://ntaugcnet.com/ugc-net-teaching-aptitude-pyq/ ; https://www.pw.live/ugc-net/exams/ugc-net-paper-1-most-scoring-topics-2026 ; https://oswaalbooks.com/blogs/entrance-examination/most-repeated-questions-in-ugc-net-paper-1-topic-wise
- **Item style:** several sources say items have shifted toward classroom scenarios ("which kind of assessment is this teacher using?") and toward statement-based, match and assertion-reason items. Sources: https://blog.ipleaders.in/ugc-net-paper-1-previous-year-question/ ; https://lawsikho.com/blog/ugc-net-paper-1-previous-year-questions/
