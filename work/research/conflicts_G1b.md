## Booklet items: dated, ambiguous or partly confirmed
- S1-Q5 (key C, Stanford 1963). Stanford and Dwight W. Allen confirmed. The year is not settled: Wikipedia gives 1963, Allen's own essay (redined.educacion.gob.es) says he began at Stanford in 1959, and another source says 1961. The answer (Stanford) is unaffected. Guide says "the year usually given is 1963".
- S7-Q21, S18-Q24 (Indian model 6-6-12-6-6 = 36 min). Timings confirmed only from secondary sources (slides, an NCERT-based college document, a B.Ed. entrance item). The Passi/NCERT original was not reachable. One NCERT-based document gives the lesson as 5-10 minutes rather than 6. Guide teaches 36 minutes, consistent with the booklet.
- S16-Q37 (Indian vs Stanford model). Stanford's videotaping confirmed; the Indian model's "peers as pupils, no costly equipment" not confirmed from a primary source.
- S15-Q11 (presage variables). Answer (presage) confirmed from Dunkin and Biddle (1974) secondary sources. The booklet explanation also names Mitzel; this attribution could not be confirmed and the guide omits Mitzel. Search sources describe the Dunkin and Biddle model as presage-process-product; the "context" variables (pupils, school, community) in the brief and the guide could not be confirmed this session, though the guide teaches them because S15-Q11's distractor needs them. Recheck.
- S6-Q21 explanation pairs "Flanders and Amidon" with interaction analysis. Amidon not verified; guide says only "Flanders with interaction analysis".
- S18-Q36 (NEP 2020). 50 hours CPD confirmed (NCERT CIET brochure quoting the policy; Lok Sabha reply). The four-year integrated B.Ed. by 2030 confirmed only from news sources; NEP PDF blocked. Both taught in a Fact watch box with "checked October 2026".

- S6-Q40 / S16-Q39 / S13-Q3 flag (analytic method unknown-to-known vs maxim known-to-unknown): handled with an explicit Often-confused entry in 1.6; no conflict with the keys.
- S12-Q41 (wait time "three seconds or more"). Confirmed from secondary sources; one source puts the threshold near 2.7 seconds. Guide says "about three seconds or more".

Confidentiality of examination work and of student information are taught as general professional duties, not quoted from the code.
