::front Facts to Recheck before Your Examination
The facts below can change when a ministry, the UGC or the NTA issues a new notice; every one was checked in October 2026, and each line gives the position printed in this guide and the official place to check it.
- **SWAYAM PRABHA channels** (4.6). Began with 32 channels in 2017. In 2026 official sources disagreed: two PIB documents (March and June 2026) gave 48, while the SWAYAM PRABHA website and a PIB factsheet of August 2026 gave 40. Check the SWAYAM PRABHA website and the latest PIB release.
- **SWAYAM National Coordinators** (4.6). Ten, including INI (Institutions of National Importance, coordinated by Banaras Hindu University); older sources list nine. Check the SWAYAM website and the Ministry of Education's website.
- **UGC credit-transfer limit for SWAYAM** (4.6). Up to 40 per cent of the total courses in a programme in a semester, under the UGC Regulations of 2021; the 2016 Regulations allowed 20 per cent. Check UGC's website.
- **Definition of a credit** (6.7). CCFUP (December 2022): 15 hours of theory or 30 hours of practical per semester for one credit. NCrF: 30 notional learning hours for one credit, 1,200 hours a year for 40 credits. Check UGC's website.
- **NHEQF levels** (6.7). Higher education qualifications on levels 4.5 to 8, with the doctorate at level 8. Check UGC's website.
- **NAAC's accreditation framework** (3.7). Under revision; do not rely on the grading scale in older books. Check NAAC's website.
- **One Nation One Subscription** (4.6). Operational from 1 January 2025, implemented by INFLIBNET; about 13,000 e-journals of 30 publishers. Check INFLIBNET's website.
- **Mode of the examination** (6.8). Computer-based in normal practice since the NTA took over the UGC NET in 2018, but fixed afresh each time. Check the NTA information bulletin.
- **NEP 2020 targets with dates** (1.10). At least 50 hours of continuous professional development a year for every teacher; by 2030 a four-year integrated B.Ed. as the minimum degree qualification for school teaching. Check the Ministry of Education's website.
- **RPwD Act, 2016** (2.9). 21 specified disabilities; not less than 5 per cent of seats in government and government-aided higher education, with 5 years' upper age relaxation; not less than 4 per cent of vacancies in government employment. Check the Government of India's published text of the Act.
If an official source now says something different, follow the official source.
